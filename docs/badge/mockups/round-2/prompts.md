# Round 2 — on-brand directions

Model: Seedream 5.0 Flash, 1k, 3:4, 0.5 credits each (4 total). Reference: `../reference/todalo-logo.png` (the logo SVG rendered to PNG).

Shared suffix:

> Match the Todalo brand: strictly monochrome, warm off-white #f5f4f1 background, near-black ink #111, flat and precise, hypermodern Swiss graphic design, generous empty space. At most one single small element in muted red #c0392b. No green, no gradients of colour, no photorealism, no fantasy, no watermark.
