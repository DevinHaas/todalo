# Member badge mockups — prompts

Model: **Seedream 5.0 Flash** (`seedream_5_0_flash`, 0.5 credits/image, 1k, 3:4).
Reference image (all runs): `reference/juniper-b.jpg`, "Dwarf Japanese Juniper, National Bonsai & Penjing Museum" by Sarah Stierch, CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons. Used for bonsai anatomy only (deadwood, trunk movement, pad structure).

## Shared style (appended to every prompt)

> Use the reference only for bonsai anatomy. Studio product render, museum vitrine, hypermodern and restrained. Monochrome palette: warm off-white #f5f4f1 backdrop, ink and graphite greys, near-black accents; at most one tiny hint of muted red. Soft volumetric fog, shallow depth of field, physically based materials, octane-style render. No fantasy, no saturated colour, no watermark.

Scene prompts 01–06 also end with "No text."

### 07 — badge card
> A vertical membership card, rounded corners, laid flat on a warm off-white surface. Top 70% is a framed 3D render window: a semi-cascade juniper bonsai in a hexagonal graphite pot, soft fog, small glowing white motes drifting, light from upper right. Below the window, a clean Swiss-style typographic footer on off-white: large bold "#0042" in near-black, beneath it the name "Ada Lovelace" in small grey sans-serif, the word "TODALO" small in the corner, one thin red accent line.

### 08 — badge card
> A vertical membership card with a matte near-black frame, rounded corners. Large inset 3D render window: a compact bonsai with bleached deadwood in a shallow round stone pot, dense fog, backlit god rays, tiny luminous moths. Beneath, minimal off-white typographic panel: large bold "#1337" in black, name "Kai Nakamura" in small grey sans-serif, small "TODALO MEMBER" label, a single small red dot accent.
