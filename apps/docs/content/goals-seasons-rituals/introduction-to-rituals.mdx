Plans work best when you come back to them. Rituals are short, regular moments to plan the day, rate how it went and look back on the week or the Season. They are all optional: you choose which ones you use, and goals, time blocks and the Coach work the same without any of them.

## The four rituals

| Ritual | When | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Check-in** | Each morning, 09:00 by default | Plan today: schedule what matters from your Inbox, overdue and upcoming tasks. |
| **Evening review** | Each evening, 20:00 by default | Rate today's progress on each goal in the current season. |
| **Weekly review** | Once a week, Sunday at 17:00 by default | Look back on each goal's week and plan the next one. |
| **Season review** | When a season ends | Look back on the season, choose which goals continue and set next season's targets. |

Every ritual starts switched off.

## Turn rituals on or off

To choose your rituals:

1. Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.
2. Click **Rituals**.
3. Turn on each ritual you want. Every change saves at once.
4. Set its time. For the **Weekly review**, choose the day as well.

The rituals you turn on appear in a **Rituals** section of the sidebar, with their time, or **Done** or **Skipped** once you've finished or skipped them. Click one to start it. The **Check-in** and the **Evening review** also show on your schedule as a recurring item each day, and on Google Calendar if you've connected it. Opening that item starts the ritual.

<Note>Every ritual has a **Skip** button. Skipping records that you skipped it for that day or week. Closing a ritual instead keeps nothing, so you can come back to it later.</Note>

## Plan your day with the Check-in

The Check-in opens the Today view as a calendar, with the Plan sidebar beside it titled **Check-in**. The sidebar lists overdue tasks, tasks due today or in the next 7 days without a time, and tasks without a date by horizon. Tasks that serve a goal come first.

For each task, do one of these:

- Drag it onto a time in the calendar.
- Hover over it, click the calendar button (**Schedule**) and click **Schedule today**, or give it a **Due date**.
- Click the three dots, then **Leave for now** to drop it from this Check-in.

The bar at the top fills as you go. When you're happy with the day, click **Done** at the bottom of the sidebar. See [Plan your day with the Today view](/organise-views/plan-your-day-with-the-today-view) for more on the calendar and the Plan sidebar.

## Rate your day with the Evening review

The Evening review opens in a sidebar on the right and takes one step per goal in the current season. For each goal, it asks how much today moved you towards it. Choose one rating:

- **A massive amount**
- **Solid**
- **Mixed**
- **Little progress**
- **Not relevant**

You can add **Context**: type what happened, or click **Speak** and say it, then click **Stop**. Click **Next** to move on, or **Skip goal** to leave a goal unrated. After the last goal, click **Finish** to save today's ratings.

Your ratings feed goal status, the Weekly review and the Season review. See [Track progress with milestones and goal status](/goals-seasons-rituals/track-progress-with-milestones-and-goal-status).

## Look back with the Weekly review

The Weekly review also takes one step per goal. Above each rating, it shows the goal's week: **Tasks completed**, **Time scheduled**, **Time on completed tasks** and the **Daily ratings** from your Evening reviews. Rate the week the same way you rate a day.

The last step asks "Do you want to plan next week now?":

- **Yes, plan next week** opens next week on the Upcoming view as a calendar, with the Plan sidebar beside it.
- **No, I've got a clear idea** finishes the review.

If you've connected an AI provider, you can also click **Plan a goal with the Coach**. The Coach may also point out tasks nobody has touched for 30 days or more and offer **Tidy up**, and after each review it learns patterns about how you work.

<Note>Goal status stays **Too early** until you complete your first Weekly review of the season. If you want to see whether your goals are on track, keep the Weekly review on.</Note>

## Close a season with the Season review

The Season review becomes available once a season is running, and is marked **Due** in the sidebar on the season's last day. The Season page then shows a **Start Season review** button.

For each goal, it shows your ratings this season and asks how much the whole season moved you towards it. The last step, "Which goals continue?", has the same choices as ending a season: the next season's dates, and the goals that carry over with their tier and next target. Finishing ends the season, keeps it as history and starts the next. See [Plan a season](/goals-seasons-rituals/plan-a-season).

| Start the Check-in | <Shortcut keys="Shift+C" /> |
| Start the Evening review | <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="E" /> |
| Start the Weekly review | <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="W" /> |
| Start the Season review | <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="R" /> |
| Next step, or finish | <Shortcut keys="Alt+Shift+N" /> |

If a ritual is switched off, its shortcut shows a message with a link to **Settings** › **Rituals** instead.

## Get notified when a ritual is ready

With **Notify me when a ritual is ready** on in **Settings** › **Rituals**, Todalo sends a notification at a ritual's time, unless you've already done or skipped it: once per ritual each day, or each week for the Weekly review. Click the notification to open the ritual. It's on by default. The Season review has no notification; the Season page tells you when it's due.

Notifications only arrive while Todalo is open. The first time you turn on a ritual, your browser or Mac asks whether Todalo may send notifications. On a Mac, see [Allow notifications and the microphone on Mac](/apps-account/allow-notifications-and-the-microphone-on-mac).
