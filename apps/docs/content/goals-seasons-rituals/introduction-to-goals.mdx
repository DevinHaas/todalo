A long list of tasks says what to do, not why. A goal is an outcome you're working towards, such as running a half marathon or finishing a book. You link projects and tasks to it, and Todalo shows you what serves each goal and how it's going.

## How goals fit with projects and tasks

A goal sits above your projects. One goal can be served by several projects, and one task can count towards more than one goal. A task called `Eat enough protein` in a project called Run marathon could serve both "Become fit" and "Build muscle".

A task serves a goal in one of two ways:

- Through its project. Link a project to a goal, and every task in that project serves the goal.
- Directly. Link a single task to a goal, whatever project it's in.

Goals are optional. If you don't use them, projects and tasks work exactly the same.

## Create a goal

Goals live in the **Goals** section at the top of the sidebar. To create one:

1. Click the plus button (**Add goal**) next to **Goals** in the sidebar.
2. Type a name, for example `Run a half marathon`.
3. Click **Add**. The goal's page opens.

Each new goal gets the next colour in the palette and a target icon. You can change both.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Shift+Q" /> to create a goal from anywhere.</Tip>

To change the order of your goals, drag one up or down in the sidebar. The order matters: when a task serves more than one goal, it takes the colour of the first one in the list.

## Edit a goal

Open a goal from the sidebar to see its page. To change its details:

1. Click **Edit** at the top right of the goal's page.
2. Change the **Title** or the **Description**. The description is a good place to say why the goal matters and what reaching it looks like.
3. Pick a **Colour** and an **Icon**.
4. Click **Save goal**.

The colour and icon show wherever the goal appears: in the sidebar, beside linked tasks, and on time blocks in your schedule.

## Link a project to a goal

Linking a project is the quickest way to connect a lot of work. To link one:

1. Open the project.
2. Click **Goals** at the top of the project.
3. Tick each goal the project serves. Each tick saves at once.

The button then shows how many goals the project serves, for example "Goals · 2". Untick a goal to remove the link.

## Link a task to a goal

To link a single task:

1. Click the task's name. The task opens.
2. Next to **Goals** on the right, click the plus button (**Link goals**).
3. Tick each goal the task serves.

Linked goals show as small chips under **Goals**. Click them to change the links.

While a task has no goal, Todalo may suggest one under the **Goals** field, for example "Suggested: Run a half marathon". Click **Link** to accept it, or click the cross to dismiss it. Nothing is linked until you click. If you've connected an AI provider, the Coach can also suggest goals by what a task means, shown as "Coach suggests".

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Shift+L" /> on a task to open its details on the **Goals** field.</Tip>

## What a goal's page shows

A goal's page brings together everything that serves it:

- **Milestones**: the tasks marked as milestones for this goal, and how many of their done criteria are ticked.
- **Projects**: the projects linked to the goal.
- **Tasks**: the tasks linked directly to the goal.

To remove a link, hover over a project or task and click the cross at the right of its row (**Unlink**). Only the link goes; the project or task stays.

If the goal is part of the current Season, a card at the top shows whether it is **Primary** or **Secondary** this season, its target, and its goal status. Click **Season** on the card to open the Season page. See [Plan a season](/goals-seasons-rituals/plan-a-season) and [Track progress with milestones and goal status](/goals-seasons-rituals/track-progress-with-milestones-and-goal-status).

If you've connected an AI provider, the page also has **Plan with the Coach**, which starts a planning conversation about this goal. See [Introduction to the Coach](/coach/introduction-to-the-coach).

## Archive a goal

When a goal no longer matters, archive it instead of letting it clutter the sidebar. Click **Archive** at the top right of the goal's page. The goal leaves the sidebar but keeps its links and history, and its page says "This goal is archived. It is hidden from the sidebar and keeps its data."

To bring it back, open the archived goal's page and click **Restore**.

## Hide goals and seasons

If you don't want to work with goals, you can turn them off:

1. Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.
2. Click **General**.
3. Turn off **Show goals and seasons**.

This hides the Goals and Seasons sections of the sidebar, the goal fields on tasks and projects, and milestone flags. Your goals and links are kept, and they come back when you turn the switch on again.
