Plenty of tasks have no date: ideas, someday projects, things you'll get to when there's room. Without a date they pile up in one long list. A Horizon says when you mean to get to a task: **Now**, **Next** or **Later**.

## The three horizons

| Horizon | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Now** | The next things you want to plan. |
| **Next** | Tasks you'll get to after that. This is where most sorted tasks sit. |
| **Later** | Tasks you're parking. They stay in your lists, dimmed, but don't compete for your attention. |

A task in the Inbox that you haven't given a horizon is **Not sorted**. A task in a project without a horizon counts as **Next**, so you only need to sort project tasks you want in **Now** or **Later**.

## Give a task a horizon

### Use the task's details

1. Click the task's name. The task opens.
2. Click **Horizon** on the right.
3. Choose **Now**, **Next** or **Later**. The change saves at once.

To clear the horizon, choose **Not sorted**, or **Next (project default)** for a task in a project.

### Use the right-click menu

Right-click a task in any list, point to **Horizon**, and choose a horizon.

### Use the keyboard

Focus a task, then press <Shortcut keys="H" /> followed by the horizon's key:

| Horizon | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Now | <Shortcut keys="H" /> then <Shortcut keys="N" /> |
| Next | <Shortcut keys="H" /> then <Shortcut keys="X" /> |
| Later | <Shortcut keys="H" /> then <Shortcut keys="L" /> |

<Tip>To sort several tasks at once, click **Select**, tick the tasks, then click **Horizon** in the toolbar and choose one. See [Add or manage multiple tasks](/tasks-capture/add-or-manage-multiple-tasks).</Tip>

## Plan from your horizons

Horizons show in the Plan sidebar beside the calendar on the Today and Upcoming views. Below the dated tasks (**Overdue**, **Today** and **Next 7 days**), tasks without a date are grouped under **Now**, **Next**, **Inbox** (tasks not sorted yet) and **Later**.

**Now** starts open; **Next**, **Inbox** and **Later** start collapsed. Click a heading to open or close it, and Todalo remembers your choice. Within each group, tasks for your primary goals come first, then tasks for secondary goals, then the rest by priority and age.

Drag a task from the Plan sidebar onto the calendar to give it a time. See [Plan your day with the Today view](/organise-views/plan-your-day-with-the-today-view).

<Note>If the tasks in **Now** need more time than you have free this week, the Plan sidebar says so, for example "Now has 14 h; about 9 h are free this week." A task without an estimate counts as 30 minutes.</Note>

## What happens to Later tasks

A **Later** task stays in its project or the Inbox, dimmed and marked **Later**, so you can still find it. When you talk to the Coach, it doesn't plan or propose Later tasks unless you name them.

Horizons are for tasks without a date. When you give a task a date, it shows by its date like any other task and keeps its horizon. If you take the date off again, it goes back to its horizon. A **Later** task with a date comes back into view when it's due.

## Keep your horizons tidy

A quick sort now and then keeps the lists honest:

- Move a task to **Later** when you know you won't get to it soon, instead of deleting it.
- Keep **Now** short enough to fit the week.
- If you use the Check-in, it brings your horizons into the morning's plan. See [Introduction to rituals](/goals-seasons-rituals/introduction-to-rituals).
- If you've connected an AI provider, ask the Coach to "Tidy up my backlog". It drafts a change for each task nobody has touched for 30 days, such as parking it in **Later**, and nothing changes until you accept. See [Review the Coach's drafts](/coach/review-the-coachs-drafts).
