Goals without a deadline tend to drift. A Season is a stretch of time, usually a few weeks to three months, with a short list of goals that get your focus. Each goal gets a target for the season and a tier: Primary goals get your best time, Secondary goals are the first to give way when time runs short.

## Start a season

Click **Start a season** under **Seasons** in the sidebar. The setup asks one question per step, in five steps: Focus, Length, Goals, Starting point and Review. Nothing is saved until the last step, so you can go **Back** at any point.

<Steps>
<Step>Under "What should this season be about?", describe the season in your own words, for example `Get fit enough for a half marathon in March, and finish my essay collection.` You can click an example such as **Get fit for a race** to start from it. Click **Continue**.</Step>
<Step>Under "How long should it run?", choose **4 weeks**, **6 weeks**, **8 weeks** or **12 weeks**. Under **Starts**, choose **Today**, **Next Monday** or **Pick a date**. Todalo shows the dates the season runs. Click **Continue**.</Step>
<Step>Under "Which goals does it focus on?", switch on the goals for this season and choose **Primary** or **Secondary** for each. Click **Continue**.</Step>
<Step>Under "Where are you starting from?", describe where each goal stands today: what you already do, what's in the way. Leave any you're unsure of empty. Click **Continue**.</Step>
<Step>Check the summary under "Your season", then click **Start season**. The Season page opens.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="S" /> to open the Season page from anywhere.</Tip>

## Choose the goals

The Goals step lists the goals you already have, switched off. Each one you switch on needs a target: type what done looks like by the season's end, for example `Run 21 km without stopping`.

To add a goal you don't have yet, click **Add a goal**, type the goal and its target, then click **Add**. If you have no goals at all, this form opens on its own.

Around three primary goals is the guideline. With more than three, Todalo warns that they "compete for the same best hours". It's a warning, not a limit.

### With the Coach

If you've connected an AI provider, the Coach reads what you wrote in the first step and suggests up to four goals, each with a target and a tier. Suggested goals are marked with a sparkle and start switched on; your own goals are listed below them. Switch off any suggestion you don't want, or click **Suggest again** for new ones. If you change the focus or the length and come back, the Coach suggests again.

In the Starting point step, the Coach also asks one question per suggested goal about where you stand today. Your answers are kept with the goal, and the Coach plans from them.

### Without the Coach

Without a connected AI provider, the setup works the same, without suggestions: you switch on your own goals and add new ones by hand. If the Coach can't answer while you set up, Todalo says "The Coach could not answer. Pick or add the goals yourself, or try again."

## Change a running season

The Season page shows how far through the season you are, for example "Day 12 of 84 · 72 days left", and lists your goals under **Primary** and **Secondary**. Every change saves at once.

- Change a goal's tier. Click **Primary** or **Secondary** on the goal's row.
- Move a target. Click the pencil (**Move target**) on the goal's row, change the text, then click **Save**. The new target replaces the old one.
- Take a goal out of the season. Turn off the switch at the left of the goal's row. The goal itself stays, with its links.
- Add a new goal. Click **New goal** below the goals. Fill in **Goal**, its **Tier** and **Target this season**, then click **Create and add**.

<Note>While a season runs, you can only add new goals to it. To bring in a goal you already have, choose it when you end this season and start the next.</Note>

If you've connected an AI provider and have primary goals, the **Capacity** section shows the Coach's estimate of the hours a week your primary goals need. Enter the hours you have under **Time you have for primary goals** and click **Save**. If your primary goals need more, the Coach suggests making one secondary or lowering a target.

## End a season

A season doesn't end on its own. When its last day comes, or earlier if your plans change:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **End season** at the top of the Season page.</Step>
<Step>To go straight into the next season, leave **Start the next season now** ticked. Choose a **Start date** and a **Length**: **12 weeks**, **4 weeks** or **Custom**.</Step>
<Step>Under **Goals that continue**, tick each goal to carry over, then choose its tier and type its **Next target**.</Step>
<Step>Click **End and start next**. If you unticked **Start the next season now**, the button says **End season**.</Step>
</Steps>

The ended season is kept as history, with the goals, tiers and targets it had. Find it under **Past seasons** on the Season page, or in the sidebar under **Seasons**.

<Tip>If you turn on the Season review ritual, it takes you through the same choices after looking back on each goal. See [Introduction to rituals](/goals-seasons-rituals/introduction-to-rituals).</Tip>
