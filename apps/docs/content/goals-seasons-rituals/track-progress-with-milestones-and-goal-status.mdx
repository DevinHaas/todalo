It's hard to tell from a list of finished tasks whether a goal is getting closer. Milestones mark the points that matter, with clear conditions for when each one is reached. Goal status then shows, for every goal in the current Season, whether you're on track, calculated from what you actually did.

## Mark a task as a milestone

A milestone is a task that marks real progress towards a goal, such as `Run 15 km` for a half marathon. The task must be linked to the goal first; see [Introduction to goals](/goals-seasons-rituals/introduction-to-goals).

To mark one:

1. Click the task's name. The task opens.
2. Turn on the **Milestone** switch on the right. While the task has no linked goal, the switch is off and says "Link this task to a goal first; a milestone marks progress toward one."
3. If the task serves more than one goal, choose which one under **Milestone for**.

Milestones show a flag in your task lists and on the goal's page.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Shift+K" /> on a task to open its details on the **Milestone** switch, then press <Shortcut keys="Space" /> to turn it on or off.</Tip>

## Add done criteria

Done criteria are the specific, checkable conditions that say when a milestone is reached, for example "Under 1 h 40 min" and "No walking breaks". When you mark a milestone, a **Done criteria** section appears in the task's details. To add one, click **Add done criterion**, type it and press Enter.

Tick each criterion as you meet it. You can edit, reorder or remove criteria in the same section.

A milestone with criteria is reached once every criterion is ticked. A milestone without criteria is reached when you complete the task. On the goal's page, each milestone shows **Reached**, how many criteria are ticked (for example "2/3"), or "No criteria".

If you complete a milestone while some of its criteria are still open, Todalo asks: "This milestone has open done criteria. Complete it and tick them all?" Click **Complete and tick all**, or **Cancel** to leave the task open.

## Read a goal's status

Every goal in the current season has one of four statuses:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **On track** | Recent weeks show steady progress. |
| **Adjust** | Progress is mixed. A change of plan now could bring the goal back on track. |
| **Off track** | Recent weeks show little progress. |
| **Too early** | There isn't enough to measure yet. |

You'll find the status as a badge beside each goal on the Season page and on the goal's page. In the sidebar, a coloured dot beside the goal shows the same status; there's no dot while a goal is **Too early**.

## How goal status is calculated

You never set a goal's status, and neither does the Coach. Todalo calculates it from four things, week by week through the season:

- Your progress ratings from the Evening review and the Weekly review. **Not relevant** ratings are left out.
- Tasks you completed that serve the goal.
- Time you kept: how much of the time you scheduled for the goal's tasks went to tasks you completed.
- Milestones you reached, counted in the week you completed the milestone's task.

A signal that's missing in a week, such as no ratings or nothing scheduled, is left out of that week instead of counting against you. Completed work always counts, so a week with nothing done scores low. The last three weeks count most, the latest week the most of all, so a good week shows quickly.

A goal is **Too early** for the first days of a season (up to a week, less for a short season) and until you complete your first Weekly review of the season.

<Note>Goal status needs at least one completed Weekly review in the season. If you don't use the Weekly review, every goal stays **Too early**. See [Introduction to rituals](/goals-seasons-rituals/introduction-to-rituals).</Note>

## Follow the trajectory

The **Trajectory** section of the Season page draws a line for each goal, one point per week, against the On track, Adjust and Off track bands. A dashed line marks today, and from there a dashed projection shows where the goal will be at the season's end if the recent trend holds.

Click a goal's name under the chart to open its page. Until your first Weekly review, the section says: "Your trajectory appears after your first Weekly review, once a week of ratings and tasks has been measured. Until then every goal shows Too early."

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Alt+Shift+G" /> on the Season page to move to the chart's goals, then Tab between them.</Tip>

## Get help from the Coach

If you've connected an AI provider, the Coach adds two things:

- An explanation under each measured goal's status, in plain words, on the Season page and the goal's page.
- A recommendation on the Season page for each goal at **Adjust** or **Off track**. Click **Update schedule** and the Coach drafts a week that carries it out, which opens on Upcoming for you to review. Click **Chat with coach** to talk it through, or click the cross to dismiss the recommendation.

Nothing changes until you accept the Coach's draft. See [Review the Coach's drafts](/coach/review-the-coachs-drafts).
