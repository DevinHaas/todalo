Todalo starts in a plain light theme, and you can make it look the way you like: dark or light, tinted colours, a different font, a wallpaper behind see-through panels. It also plays a few quiet sounds, which you can change or turn off. Your look is saved to your account, so it follows you to every device you sign in on.

## Change the look

To change how Todalo looks:

<Steps>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Appearance**.</Step>
<Step>Choose a mode under **Mode**: **Light**, **Dark** or **System**, which follows your computer's light or dark appearance.</Step>
<Step>Click one of the ready-made designs below the colours, for example **Sage**, **Terminal** or **Harbor**, or set each part yourself. Todalo changes as you go.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="O" /> and then <Shortcut keys="T" /> to switch between light and dark from anywhere in the app.</Tip>

A ready-made design sets the colours, accent, font, corners, borders and glass in one click and leaves your mode alone. Hover over a design to see its name; the name of the one you're using shows next to them, or "Custom" once you change something.

### What you can change

| Setting | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Background** | **Colour** or **Image**: one at a time. Todalo keeps both, so switching back brings your colours or picture back. |
| **Colours** | On the **Colour** tab: the tint of the sidebar and page. Drag a colour around the field, or click **Add a colour (a gradient)** for up to three. |
| **Intensity** | How strongly the colours tint the sidebar and page. **Off** is the plain theme. |
| **Grain** | A film-like texture over the colours. |
| **Accent** | Buttons, links, selection and today's dates. Choose a swatch, such as **Todalo red** or **Blue**, or **Any colour**. |
| **Font** | **System**, **Geist**, **Source Serif**, **JetBrains Mono** or **IBM Plex Mono**. |
| **Corners** | **Sharp**, **Soft** or **Round**. |
| **Borders** | **None**, **Subtle** or **Strong**, plus a **Border colour** of your own. |
| **Glass** | How much the wallpaper, or your colours, show through the sidebar, page and dialogs. |

## Add a wallpaper

To put a picture behind Todalo:

<Steps>
<Step>In **Settings** › **Appearance**, open the **Image** tab under **Background** and click **Choose an image**.</Step>
<Step>Choose a JPEG, PNG or WebP image. Large images are made smaller to fit.</Step>
<Step>Drag **Glass** to choose how much of the wallpaper shows through the panels.</Step>
</Steps>

Todalo switches to the **Image** background and turns on some glass when you add a wallpaper, because the picture only shows through glass. The **Colour** tab hides the picture without deleting it. To use another picture, click **Replace**; to take it away, click the cross next to it (**Remove the wallpaper**).

<Note>Todalo's window itself isn't see-through: your desktop doesn't show behind it. A wallpaper with glass gives the same look on the web and on Mac.</Note>

## Share a design

Each design has a design code, a line of text you can send to someone or keep for later. Under **Design code**, click **Copy** to copy yours. To use a code, paste it into **Paste a design code** and click **Apply**. **Reset** goes back to Todalo's own look and keeps your mode.

To go further than these settings, you can restyle Todalo with a stylesheet of your own. See [Use custom CSS](/apps-account/customize-with-css).

## Choose your sounds

Todalo has two sounds you can choose: one when you complete a task and one when the Coach opens. To choose them:

<Steps>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Click **General**. Check that **Sound effects** is switched on.</Step>
<Step>Open the **Task completed** menu and choose a sound. Todalo plays it, so you can try a few before you decide.</Step>
<Step>Open the **Coach opens** menu and choose a sound the same way.</Step>
</Steps>

Choose **None** in either menu to silence that one sound and keep the others.

### Sounds you can choose

| Sound | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Task completed** | **Bubble plop** (the default), **Double bubble**, **Bright plop**, **Soft pop**, **Pop**, **Ringing pop**, **Suction pop**, **Can opening**, **Click**, **Fizz pop**, **Glass chime**, **Completed**, **Chime 1**, **Chime 2**, **Chime 3**, **Mission complete 1**, **Mission complete 2**, **Mission complete 3**, **None** |
| **Coach opens** | **Magic** (the default), **Magic swell**, **Check-in**, **Bloom**, **Rise**, **None** |

Ramble's sounds, which tell you when the microphone is ready, when a task is added and when your tasks are saved, can't be changed. They're on and off with the rest.

## Turn all sounds off

To silence Todalo, click your name, then **Settings** › **General**, and switch off **Sound effects**. The sound menus disappear until you switch it on again, and your choices are kept.

<Note>Sounds are on or off per device, unlike your look. Turning them off on your Mac leaves them on in your browser, and the other way round.</Note>
