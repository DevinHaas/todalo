Todalo comes in a light theme and a dark theme, and plays a few quiet sounds when you complete a task, open the Coach and use Ramble. You can switch the theme, pick the sounds you like or turn them off. Both are remembered on the device you set them on.

## Switch to the dark theme

To switch the theme:

<Steps>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Theme**.</Step>
<Step>Switch on **Dark theme**. Todalo changes straight away; switch it off to go back to light.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="O" /> and then <Shortcut keys="T" /> to switch between light and dark from anywhere in the app.</Tip>

Todalo doesn't follow your computer's light or dark appearance. It starts in the light theme and stays in whichever theme you last chose, even when your system switches at sunset.

<Note>The theme is saved on each device, not in your account. If you use Todalo on the web and on your Mac, set the theme on each.</Note>

## Choose your sounds

Todalo has two sounds you can choose: one when you complete a task and one when the Coach opens. To choose them:

<Steps>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Click **General**. Check that **Sound effects** is switched on.</Step>
<Step>Open the **Task completed** menu and choose a sound. Todalo plays it, so you can try a few before you decide.</Step>
<Step>Open the **Coach opens** menu and choose a sound the same way.</Step>
</Steps>

Choose **None** in either menu to silence that one sound and keep the others.

### Sounds you can choose

| Sound | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Task completed** | **Bubble plop** (the default), **Double bubble**, **Bright plop**, **Soft pop**, **Pop**, **Ringing pop**, **Suction pop**, **Can opening**, **Click**, **Fizz pop**, **Glass chime**, **Completed**, **Chime 1**, **Chime 2**, **Chime 3**, **Mission complete 1**, **Mission complete 2**, **Mission complete 3**, **None** |
| **Coach opens** | **Magic** (the default), **Magic swell**, **Check-in**, **Bloom**, **Rise**, **None** |

Ramble's sounds, which tell you when the microphone is ready, when a task is added and when your tasks are saved, can't be changed. They're on and off with the rest.

## Turn all sounds off

To silence Todalo, click your name, then **Settings** › **General**, and switch off **Sound effects**. The sound menus disappear until you switch it on again, and your choices are kept.

<Note>Sounds are on or off per device. Turning them off on your Mac leaves them on in your browser, and the other way round.</Note>
