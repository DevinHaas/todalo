Most sign-in problems come down to a typing mistake, the wrong sign-in method or a dropped connection. Find the message you see below and follow the steps for it. Your tasks stay safe in your account while you sort it out.

## Fix a wrong email or password

If Todalo shows "Could not sign in. Check your email and password.":

1. Check the email for typing mistakes, and that it's the address you signed up with.
2. Check that Caps Lock is off, and type the password again.
3. If you created your account with Google, there's no password to type. Click **Continue with Google** instead.

## Get back in if you forgot your password

You can't reset a password from the sign-in screen yet, and Todalo has no way to change one in Settings either. Write to us at the address below, from the email address on your account, and we'll help you back in.

If you linked Google to your account, you can still sign in with **Continue with Google** while you wait.

## Sign in with Google when you already have a password

If you see "This email already has a todalo account. Sign in with your password, then link Google from your account.", you signed up with an email and password, and Todalo won't join the two on its own. To use Google from now on:

<Steps>
<Step>Sign in with your email and password.</Step>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Calendars**, then **Link another Google account**, and finish in Google.</Step>
</Steps>

After that, **Continue with Google** signs you in to this account.

## Use a Google account that's linked elsewhere

"That Google account is already linked to a different todalo account." means this Google account already signs in to another Todalo account. Sign in to that account with **Continue with Google** to check which one it is, or link a different Google account.

## Finish Google sign-in

Google sign-in has to be finished within 10 minutes. If it takes longer, Todalo shows "Google sign-in took too long. Try again." Click **Continue with Google** to start again.

<Platform on="web">
On the web, Google sends you back to Todalo in the same tab. If you land on the sign-in screen without being signed in, click **Continue with Google** again and finish choosing your account.
</Platform>

<Platform on="mac">
On Mac, sign-in continues in your default browser while Todalo shows "Continue in your browser, then return to todalo."

1. Finish choosing your Google account in the browser.
2. When the browser offers to open Todalo, allow it.
3. If Todalo still waits, click **Cancel** in Todalo, then click **Continue with Google** to start again.

If you closed the browser tab before finishing, click **Cancel** and start again.
</Platform>

If you see "Google sign-in could not be completed. Try again.", start again from **Continue with Google**. If it keeps failing, sign in with your email and password if you have one.

## Check your connection

"Cannot reach todalo. Check your connection and try again." means Todalo couldn't reach its server. Check that you're online and that other websites load, then try again. If you're online and it keeps happening, wait a few minutes: Todalo may be briefly unavailable.

## Sign in again after being signed out

If Todalo shows "Please sign in again." or opens on the sign-in screen by itself, your session on this device has ended. Sign in as usual. Nothing in your account is lost, and your other devices stay signed in.

## Fix Apple Keychain errors on Mac

The Mac app keeps your session in your Mac's Keychain. If you see "Apple Keychain is unavailable. Unlock your device and try again." or "Your session could not be saved in Apple Keychain. Try again.", make sure you're logged in to your Mac user account and the Mac is unlocked, then sign in again.

If you see "The saved credential is invalid. Sign out and try again.", click your name at the top left of the sidebar, click **Log out**, then sign in again.

## Create an account with an email that's taken

"Could not create your account. Check your details or sign in if you already have an account." usually means the email already has a Todalo account. Click **Sign in** at the bottom of the screen and sign in instead, or use **Continue with Google** if that's how you signed up.
