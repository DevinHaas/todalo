Each Todalo release comes with a short list of what changed. This page lists every release, newest first, with the same notes the app shows.

To see them in the app, click your name at the top left of the sidebar and click **What's new** for the latest release. For every release, click **Changelog** at the bottom of the same menu, next to your version number. Todalo also opens **What's new** once after it updates.
