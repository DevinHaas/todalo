Almost everything in Todalo has a keyboard shortcut, from adding a task to starting a Ritual. You can look them up without leaving the app, run any command by typing its name, and change any shortcut you'd rather press differently. Your shortcuts are saved to your account, so they follow you to every device.

## See every shortcut in the app

To open the shortcut list, press <Shortcut keys="?" />, or click your name at the top left of the sidebar and click **Keyboard shortcuts**.

The list shows your shortcuts, including any you've changed, grouped by area. Type in **Search shortcuts…** to find one by its action or its keys. Press <Shortcut keys="Esc" /> to close it.

## Run a command by name

The command palette runs any command without a shortcut to remember. To use it:

1. Press <Shortcut keys="Mod+P" />.
2. Start typing what you want to do, for example `today` or `theme`.
3. Use the arrow keys to choose a command, then press <Shortcut keys="Enter" />.

Each command shows its shortcut, so the palette is also a quick way to learn them. To keep a command at the top of the list, point at it and click its pin button, or press <Shortcut keys="Alt+P" /> while it's highlighted.

<Note>In Todalo, <Shortcut keys="Mod+P" /> opens the command palette instead of printing the page.</Note>

## Use sequences

Some shortcuts are two keys pressed one after the other rather than together. For example, press <Shortcut keys="G" /> and then <Shortcut keys="T" /> to go to the Today view. You have about a second to press the second key.

Single-key shortcuts and sequences don't work while you're typing in a field, so you can write a task called "get groceries" without jumping around the app.

## Change a shortcut

To change a shortcut:

<Steps>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Keyboard shortcuts**.</Step>
<Step>Find the action. Type in **Search shortcuts…**, or use the **All**, **Assigned**, **Custom** and **Unassigned** filters.</Step>
<Step>Click the action's current shortcut, or **Add Hotkey** if it has none.</Step>
<Step>Press the new keys. A combination with <Shortcut keys="Mod" />, <Shortcut keys="Ctrl" /> or <Shortcut keys="Alt" /> saves at once. For a sequence, press up to three keys one after the other, then wait a moment.</Step>
</Steps>

The change saves to your account straight away. To give an action a second shortcut, point at its row and click **Add**. To remove a shortcut, click it, then click the cross next to it.

If the keys you pressed already belong to another action, Todalo shows which one and asks whether to replace it. Click **Replace** to move the shortcut to this action, or **Cancel** to keep things as they were.

Some keys can't be used. <Shortcut keys="Tab" /> always moves between controls, and combinations your browser or operating system needs, such as closing a tab or quitting, are refused with a message saying so.

<Tip>From the shortcut list, click **Customize shortcuts in your account** to go straight to these settings.</Tip>

### Set different shortcuts for each platform

By default, a change applies to every platform, and Todalo uses <Shortcut keys="Mod" /> to mean Command on a Mac and Ctrl elsewhere. To change the keys for one platform only, choose **macOS**, **Windows** or **Linux** in the menu above the list instead of **All platforms**, then change the shortcut.

### Go back to the default shortcuts

To undo all your changes, click **Reset all shortcuts** at the bottom of the list, then **Reset all**. This can't be undone, so you'd need to set your own shortcuts again.
