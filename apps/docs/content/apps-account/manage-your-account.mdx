Your account holds your tasks, projects, goals and settings, and it's what you sign in with on every device. The **Account** page in Settings shows who you're signed in as and lets you change your photo or log out.

## Open your account settings

Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, click **Settings**, then click **Account**.

The page shows your photo, your name and the email address you sign in with.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="O" /> and then <Shortcut keys="U" /> to open your account settings from anywhere.</Tip>

## Change your photo

Until you add a photo, Todalo shows the first letter of your name. To add or change your photo:

<Steps>
<Step>Open **Settings** › **Account**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Change photo**, or click the photo itself.</Step>
<Step>Choose an image. JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC images work.</Step>
</Steps>

Todalo crops the image to a square from its centre and saves a small copy to your account, so the same photo shows on every device. Pick an image with your face near the middle.

If you see "Your photo could not be saved. Try another image.", the image couldn't be read or the save failed. Try again, or choose a different image.

To go back to your initial, click **Remove** next to **Change photo**.

## Change your name, email or password

You can't change your name, your email address or your password in Todalo yet. To change any of them, write to us at the address below from the email address on your account, and we'll make the change for you.

If you signed up with Google, your name and email come from your Google account, and you sign in with **Continue with Google** rather than a password.

## Sign in with Google as well as a password

If you created your account with an email and password, you can link your Google account so that **Continue with Google** signs you in too. You link it from **Settings** › **Calendars**; see [Create your account and sign in](/get-started/create-your-account-and-sign-in) for the steps.

## What your account keeps

Your tasks, projects, labels, filters, goals, Rituals and Coach settings are saved to your account, and so are your keyboard shortcuts. They're the same wherever you sign in.

A few preferences belong to the device instead: the theme, the sounds and whether they play. See [Change your theme and sounds](/apps-account/change-your-theme-and-sounds).

## Log out

To log out, open **Settings** › **Account** and click **Log out**, or click your name at the top left of the sidebar and click **Log out**. Todalo shows the sign-in screen.

Logging out on one device leaves you signed in on your others.
