Most calendar problems come down to a calendar that isn't shown, a Google permission that was withdrawn, or a connection that dropped. Find the message or symptom you see below. Every fix starts in **Settings** › **Calendars**, which you open by clicking your name at the top left of the sidebar, then **Settings**.

## An account says "Grant calendar access to show this account's events."

Todalo can't read that account's calendars. Usually access to your calendars was not allowed when you connected, or it was removed later in your Google account settings.

To grant access again:

<Steps>
<Step>On the account's card, click **Link another Google account**.</Step>
<Step>In Google, choose the same Google account.</Step>
<Step>Allow Todalo to access your calendars.</Step>
</Steps>

When you're back in Todalo, the card lists the account's calendars again. Show the ones you want with the eye buttons.

## An account shows Error instead of Live

Each card shows **Live** when its visible calendars are up to date. **Error** means a calendar you chose to show hasn't been read from Google yet. Click **Resync** on the card. If it still says **Error**, hide the calendar and show it again with its eye button.

## Events are missing from Today or Upcoming

Check these in **Settings** › **Calendars**:

- **The calendar is shown.** New accounts start with every calendar hidden. Click the eye button next to the calendar's name.
- **The account's events are on.** **Show events in Today/Upcoming** must be on for the account.
- **The account is up to date.** Click **Resync** on the card.

Some events never appear, by design:

- Events further back than about two months, or more than about a year ahead.
- Events Todalo created from your own tasks. The task already shows in their place.

## A task doesn't appear in Google Calendar

Only tasks with a date go to Google Calendar, into the main calendar of the first Google account you connected. Todalo doesn't show a message when sending a task fails. To try again:

<Steps>
<Step>Check that the account's card doesn't ask you to grant calendar access. If it does, grant it as described above.</Step>
<Step>Change the task's name, date or time. Todalo sends the task again.</Step>
</Steps>

Tasks that already had a date when you connected are sent the next time you change them. Completed tasks that don't repeat stay in Google Calendar; delete them there if you don't want them.

## A change in Google didn't reach the task

Only the title, date and time of events created from your tasks come back to Todalo. Description changes, and events you created in Google yourself, stay in Google.

Todalo also skips a change from Google if the task changed in Todalo after it, even in a detail Google doesn't show, such as its priority. Make the change in Todalo instead; it's sent to Google if it touches the name, description, date or time.

## Your calendars could not be loaded

If **Settings** › **Calendars** shows "Your calendars could not be loaded. Try again.", click **Try again**. If it shows "Cannot reach todalo. Check your connection and try again.", check your internet connection first.

## A calendar change didn't save

When showing, hiding, resyncing or disconnecting fails, Todalo undoes the change on screen and shows a message such as "Couldn't hide the calendar". Click **Retry** in the message.

- "You're offline." means your device has no connection. Try again once you're back online.
- "Something went wrong on todalo's side." can mean Google didn't answer. Wait a moment and click **Retry**.
- "It no longer exists; it may have been deleted on another device." means the account was disconnected elsewhere. The list refreshes by itself.

## You can't disconnect a Google account

If your Todalo account has no password and this is its only Google account, that Google account is how you sign in, so Todalo can't unlink it. Clicking **Disconnect** shows "Couldn't disconnect the calendar account", and the account's calendars are hidden.

To stop seeing its events, keep the account and turn off **Show events in Today/Upcoming**, or hide its calendars.

## Linking a Google account fails

The message appears above **Link another Google account**:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "That Google account is already linked to a different todalo account." | Sign in to that other Todalo account and disconnect the Google account there, or link a different Google account. |
| "Google sign-in took too long. Try again." | Click **Link another Google account** again and finish in the browser without a long pause. |
| "Cannot open Google sign-in. Try again." | Check your connection, then try again. |
| "Google sign-in could not be completed. Try again." | Wait a moment, then click **Link another Google account** again. |

If you close Google without allowing access, nothing changes and no message appears.

## Google sign-in says to link Google from your account

If you tried **Continue with Google** and saw "This email already has a todalo account. Sign in with your password, then link Google from your account.", sign in with your email and password first. Then go to **Settings** › **Calendars** and click **Link another Google account**. That's where Todalo links Google to an existing account. For other sign-in problems, see [Troubleshoot sign-in](/apps-account/troubleshoot-sign-in).
