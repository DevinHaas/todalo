A busy Thursday is easier to handle on Monday than on Thursday morning. The Upcoming view shows your dated tasks day by day, so you can spot a crowded day early and move things before it arrives. Click **Upcoming** in the sidebar to open it.

## Read the Upcoming view

Upcoming starts at the Monday of the current week and runs a year ahead, one section per day. Each heading gives the date and the weekday, and marks today and tomorrow. Under each day you'll find:

- your Google Calendar events for that day, if you've connected a calendar;
- the tasks due that day;
- **Add task**, to add a task due that day.

Tasks from before this week that you haven't done sit under **Overdue** at the top. Tasks more than a year away sit under **Later** at the bottom.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="U" /> to go to Upcoming from anywhere.</Tip>

## Move between days and weeks

The strip at the top shows the seven days of the week you're looking at, and highlights the day at the top of the list. To move around:

- Click a day in the strip to jump to it.
- Click **Previous week** or **Next week** to move a week at a time.
- Click **Today** to come back to today.

As you scroll, the strip follows along.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Shift+ArrowLeft" /> or <Shortcut keys="Shift+ArrowRight" /> to move a week back or forward, and <Shortcut keys="Home" /> to return to today.</Tip>

## Spread out a busy week

When a day looks too full, move some of its tasks to a quieter day:

1. Click the task's name. It opens without leaving Upcoming.
2. Click **Date** on the right and pick another day.
3. Close the task. It moves to its new day in the list.

To add something for a particular day, click **Add task** under that day. The task is due that day.

<Tip>To move several tasks at once, click **Select** at the top of Upcoming, tick the tasks, and use the toolbar. See [Add or manage multiple tasks](/tasks-capture/add-or-manage-multiple-tasks).</Tip>

## Plan the week on a calendar

The calendar layout shows the week hour by hour, which helps when your tasks have times. To switch to it:

1. Click **Display** at the top right.
2. Under **Layout**, click **Calendar**, then choose **Week** or **Month**.
3. Close the dialog.

In the week calendar you can block time for tasks just as on Today: drag over an empty slot to add a task, drag a block to move it, drag its bottom edge to resize it, and drag tasks without a time in from the Plan sidebar. See [Plan your day with the Today view](/organise-views/plan-your-day-with-the-today-view) for the details.

Use **Prev** and **Next** above the calendar to change week or month, and **Today** to come back. Todalo remembers the layout for Upcoming separately from Today.

<Note>If you've connected an AI provider, the Plan sidebar beside the week calendar offers **Draft the week with the Coach**. Its blocks appear dashed until you accept them. See [Review the Coach's drafts](/coach/review-the-coachs-drafts).</Note>

## Show completed tasks

Click **Display** and switch on **Completed tasks** to see what you've already finished on each day. Switch it off to keep the list to what's still open.
