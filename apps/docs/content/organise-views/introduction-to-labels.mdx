Some things cut across projects: errands you can do while you're out, calls you can make in a spare ten minutes, anything waiting on someone else. Labels tag those tasks wherever they live, and each label has a page that lists them together. A task can have as many labels as you like.

## Create a label

To create a label:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Filters & Labels** in the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Click the plus button beside **Labels** (**Add label**).</Step>
<Step>Type a **Name**, for example `errands`, and pick a **Color**.</Step>
<Step>Switch on **Add to favorites** if you want the label in the sidebar's **Favorites**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Add**.</Step>
</Steps>

The label appears in the **Labels** list. Names can be up to 60 characters.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="L" /> to open your labels from anywhere.</Tip>

## Add a label to a task

You can add labels while you write a task or afterwards.

### While you add a task

1. Open Quick Add and type the task name.
2. Type `@` followed by the start of a label name, for example `@err`. A list of matching labels opens.
3. Press Enter to pick the highlighted label, or click one. The label shows as a chip under the name and the `@` text is removed.

To pick labels from a list instead, click the plus button at the bottom of Quick Add (**More options**) and tick them under **Labels**. Click a label chip to remove it.

<Note>Typing `@` only picks labels you've already created. To use a new label, create it on the Filters & Labels page first.</Note>

### On an existing task

1. Click the task's name. The task opens.
2. Click **Labels** on the right (it shows **Add labels** when the task has none).
3. Tick the labels you want, and untick any you don't.

<Tip>In a list, move to a task with <Shortcut keys="J" /> and <Shortcut keys="K" />, then press <Shortcut keys="L" /> to edit its labels. To label several tasks at once, click **Select**, tick the tasks, then click **Labels** in the toolbar.</Tip>

A task row shows its labels next to the tag icon.

## See every task with a label

Click **Filters & Labels** in the sidebar, then click the label's name. The label's page lists every task that has it, from any project, grouped under **Today**, **Upcoming** and **No date**.

Click **Manage labels** at the top of a label's page to go back to the list. To keep a label one click away, add it to your favourites: it then appears under **Favorites** in the sidebar.

## Edit or delete a label

On the Filters & Labels page, hover over the label to see its buttons:

- **Edit** changes the name, colour and favourite setting. Click **Save** when you're done.
- The heart adds the label to your favourites, or removes it.
- **Delete** removes the label after you confirm.

Deleting a label doesn't delete any tasks. The label is only removed from them.

## Use labels in filters

Labels are most useful inside filters. A filter can combine labels with dates, priorities and projects, for example `@errands & today` for the errands due today. See [Introduction to filters](/organise-views/introduction-to-filters).
