The same tasks can be easier to work with in different shapes: a list for getting through them, a board for moving them through stages, a calendar for seeing when they happen. Every view in Todalo has a layout you can change, and each one remembers its own. Projects also let you sort, group and filter their tasks.

## Change the layout of a project

A project's layout is saved with the project. To change it:

1. Open the project.
2. Click **Display** at the top right.
3. Under **Layout**, click **List**, **Board**, **Calendar** or **Table**.

The project changes straight away. Click outside the panel to close it.

## Change the layout of other views

The Inbox, Today, Upcoming, label pages, filter pages and search results each keep their own layout. Today can stay a list while Upcoming shows a calendar. To change one:

1. Open the view.
2. Click **Display** at the top right.
3. Under **Layout**, click **List**, **Board** or **Calendar**, then close the dialog.

You can also click the arrows at the top left (**Previous layout** and **Next layout**) to step through the layouts. The current layout's name shows beside them.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Shift+V" /> to switch layout.</Tip>

## The layouts

### List

The list shows one task per row, with its date, priority, labels, sub-task count and project. In a project, tasks are grouped under their sections. Drag a task by its handle to reorder it when **Sorting** is set to **Manual**.

### Board

In a project, the board has a column per section, plus **(No section)** for tasks without one. Drag a card to another column to move the task into that section. Click **Add section** at the end of the board to add a column.

In the Inbox, Today, Upcoming, labels, filters and search, the board has three columns instead: **To do**, **In progress** and **Done**. Drag a card between columns to change where the task stands. Dropping it on **Done** completes it.

### Calendar

The calendar places tasks on a grid of days. Choose **Week** or **Month** under the layout buttons. In the week calendar, tasks with a time show as blocks and the rest show under **All day**. Click an empty slot to add a task at that time.

On Today the calendar shows a single day. On Today and in Upcoming's week calendar you can also drag to create, move and resize blocks; for time blocking with the Plan sidebar, see [Plan your day with the Today view](/organise-views/plan-your-day-with-the-today-view).

### Table

The table is only available in projects. It lists each task with its **Date** and **Deadline** in columns, grouped by section. Click a date to change it in place.

## Sort, group and filter a project

A project's **Display** panel also controls which tasks you see and in what order. These settings are saved with the project.

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Completed tasks** | On shows completed tasks; off hides them. |
| **Grouping** | **None (sections)**, **Date**, **Deadline**, **Date added**, **Priority**, **Label** |
| **Sorting** | **Manual**, **Date**, **Deadline**, **Date added**, **Priority**, **Name** |
| **Date** | **All**, **Today**, **This week**, **Overdue**, **No date** |
| **Deadline** | **All**, **This week**, **Overdue**, **Has deadline**, **No deadline** |
| **Created** | **All**, **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** |
| **Priority** | **All**, or one priority |
| **Label** | **All**, or one of your labels |

When you change a setting, the button shows how many are changed, for example "Display: 2". Click **Reset all** to go back to the defaults; the layout stays as it is.

<Note>Sections only show when **Grouping** is **None (sections)**. Any other grouping replaces them with its own headings, and you can't add sections or drag tasks between them until you switch back.</Note>

## Show completed tasks elsewhere

In the Inbox, Today, Upcoming and the other views, click **Display** and switch on **Completed tasks**. Each view remembers the setting along with its layout.
