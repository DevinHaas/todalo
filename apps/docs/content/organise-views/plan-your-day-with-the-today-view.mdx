Most days start with more to do than fits. The Today view lists everything due today, puts anything you missed at the top, and can turn into an hour-by-hour schedule where you give each task its own slot. Click **Today** in the sidebar to open it.

## Read the Today view

The Today view has two parts:

- **Overdue**: tasks whose date has passed. Click the heading to fold the list away.
- **Today**: the tasks due today, under today's date. If you've connected Google Calendar, today's events are listed first.

The count under the title, and the number beside **Today** in the sidebar, show how many tasks are still open. When nothing is due, the view says "Nothing is due today."

Tasks for today don't show a date in their row, because it's implied. A task with a time shows the time.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="T" /> to go to Today from anywhere.</Tip>

## Add a task for today

Click **Add task** at the bottom of the Today list. A task you add here is due today, so you don't need to type a date.

## Work through your day

- To complete a task, click the circle to its left.
- To change a task, click its name. It opens without leaving Today.
- To move a task to another day, open it and change its **Date**.

To see the tasks you've already completed today, click **Display** at the top right and switch on **Completed tasks**.

## Block time for your tasks

Time blocking means giving a task its own slot in the day, so you can see whether everything fits. In Todalo a time block is simply a task with a start and end time. To switch Today to a schedule:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Display** at the top right of the Today view.</Step>
<Step>Under **Layout**, click **Calendar**.</Step>
<Step>Close the dialog. Today shows the day hour by hour, with tasks without a time under **All day**.</Step>
</Steps>

Todalo remembers the layout for the Today view, so it opens as a schedule next time.

### Add a block

Drag down over an empty part of the schedule, from the start time to the end time. Quick Add opens with that time range filled in. Type the task name and press Enter. You can also click an empty slot to add a 15-minute task there.

### Plan from the Plan sidebar

Beside the schedule, the Plan sidebar lists the tasks that don't have a time yet: **Overdue**, **Today** and **Next 7 days** for dated tasks, then **Now**, **Next**, **Inbox** and **Later** for tasks without a date (see [Sort timeless tasks into Now, Next and Later](/goals-seasons-rituals/sort-timeless-tasks-into-now-next-and-later)). The button at the top of the schedule, which reads "Plan:" and a number, shows or hides the sidebar.

To schedule a task from the sidebar:

- Drag it onto the schedule. It takes its own length, or 30 minutes if it has none.
- Or hover over it and click the calendar button (**Schedule**). Pick a time under **Today at** and click **Schedule today**. You can also set a **Due date** there instead.

The three-dot menu on a row offers **Schedule today at** followed by the next free time.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Shift+U" /> to show or hide the Plan sidebar.</Tip>

### Move, resize or remove a block

- Drag a block to move it to another time.
- Drag its bottom edge to make it longer or shorter.
- Drag it back onto the Plan sidebar to take its time away. The task keeps its day.

Blocks snap to 15-minute steps. If a task is linked to a goal, its block is in the goal's colour.

### Catch up on overdue tasks

In the Plan sidebar, click **Reschedule** beside **Overdue** to move every overdue task to today. Todalo shows "Rescheduled 3 tasks to today" with an **Undo** button, in case you change your mind.

<Note>If you've connected an AI provider, the Plan sidebar also offers **Draft my day with the Coach**. The Coach's blocks appear dashed until you accept them. See [Review the Coach's drafts](/coach/review-the-coachs-drafts).</Note>

## Switch back to the list

Click **Display** and choose **List**, or use the arrows at the top left (**Previous layout** and **Next layout**). The **Board** layout sorts today's tasks into **To do**, **In progress** and **Done** columns.
