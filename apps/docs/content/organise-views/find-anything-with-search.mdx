Once you have a few hundred tasks, scrolling stops being the quickest way to find one. Todalo has two ways to search: Quick Find, which jumps straight to a task, project or section by name, and the search page, which also looks inside task descriptions.

## Jump to something with Quick Find

To find a task, project or section by its name:

1. Click **Search** in the sidebar. The Quick Find dialog opens.
2. Start typing in **Search tasks, projects and sections**. Matches appear as you type, each marked **Task**, **Project** or **Section**.
3. Click a result. A task opens, a project opens, and a section opens its project scrolled to that section.

Press Esc, or click outside the dialog, to close it without going anywhere.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="/" />, <Shortcut keys="F" /> or <Shortcut keys="Mod+K" /> to open Quick Find from anywhere.</Tip>

Quick Find matches names only, ignores capitals, and finds your words anywhere in a name: `dent` finds "Book the dentist". It shows up to 40 results.

## Search names and descriptions

When Quick Find doesn't show what you're after, search more widely:

1. Open Quick Find and type your search.
2. Click **View all search results** at the bottom of the dialog. The **Search** page opens.
3. Read the number of results and the matching tasks below it. To try different words, change the text in **Search tasks** and click **Search**.

The search page looks for your words in each task's name and description. It finds the text exactly as you type it, so `paint samples` finds "Order paint samples" but not "samples of paint".

The page's address keeps your search, so you can reload it or bookmark it. Its tasks work like any other list: you can complete or open them, click **Select** to change several at once, or change the layout with **Display**.

## Jump to a section

To go straight to a section in any project, press <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="/" />. The dialog opens as **Navigate sections** and lists sections only. Type part of the name and click the section.

## Search inside one project or the Plan

Some places have their own search box:

- On **My Projects**, type in **Search projects** to narrow the list of projects.
- When the Plan sidebar holds a lot of tasks, it shows a search box and filter chips for goals and labels, so you can find the task you want to schedule.

## When search isn't enough

Search finds tasks by their words. To find tasks by their date, priority, label or project, for example everything due this week at work, use a filter instead. See [Introduction to filters](/organise-views/introduction-to-filters).
