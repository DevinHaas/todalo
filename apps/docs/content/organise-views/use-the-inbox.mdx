Ideas and errands rarely arrive in good order. The Inbox is where a task waits when you haven't given it a project yet, so you can write it down now and decide where it belongs later. Go through it now and then, and move each task on.

## Add tasks to the Inbox

Any task you add without choosing a project lands in the Inbox. To add one:

1. Click **Add task** at the top of the sidebar.
2. Type the task in **Task name**. Leave the project menu at the bottom of Quick Add on **Inbox**.
3. Press Enter. Todalo confirms with "Added “…” to Inbox."

You can also open the Inbox and click **Add task** at the bottom of the list.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Q" /> to open Quick Add from anywhere, or press <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="I" /> to go to the Inbox.</Tip>

## Find your way around the Inbox

Click **Inbox** in the sidebar to open it. Tasks are grouped by date:

- **Today**: tasks due today.
- **Upcoming**: tasks with any other date, including overdue ones.
- **No date**: tasks you haven't scheduled.

A sub-task stays under its parent, and the count on the parent shows how many of its sub-tasks are done.

Every task without a project lives here, whatever its date. A task in the Inbox that's due today also shows up in the Today view.

## Move a task out of the Inbox

Giving a task a project files it away. To move a task:

1. Click the task's name. The task opens.
2. Click **Project** on the right, and choose a project.
3. Close the task. It now shows in the project, and no longer in the Inbox.

To move a task to a section as well, open its page and click **Move task**. Choose a **Project** and a **Section**, then confirm.

<Tip>To move several tasks at once, click **Select** at the top of the Inbox, tick the tasks, then click **Move** in the toolbar. See [Add or manage multiple tasks](/tasks-capture/add-or-manage-multiple-tasks).</Tip>

## Give a task a date

A date puts the task on the right day in Today and Upcoming, even if it stays in the Inbox. Open the task, click **Date** on the right and pick a day. You can also type the date into the task name when you add it, for example `Return library books friday`.

## Keep the Inbox short

The Inbox works best as a holding area, not a second to-do list. A few habits help:

- Clear it once a day or once a week. Give each task a project, a date, or both.
- Delete what you no longer need. Hover over a task and click the cross at the right of its row.
- If you use goals, the Check-in can walk you through your Inbox each morning, and you can sort tasks without a date into Now, Next and Later. See [Sort timeless tasks into Now, Next and Later](/goals-seasons-rituals/sort-timeless-tasks-into-now-next-and-later).

<Note>Deleting a project doesn't delete its tasks. They move back to the Inbox.</Note>
