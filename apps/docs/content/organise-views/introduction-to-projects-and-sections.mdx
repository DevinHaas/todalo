Most tasks belong to something bigger: a room you're redecorating, a race you're training for, your job. A project keeps those tasks together, and sections split a project into stages or areas. Each task belongs to one project at most; a task without a project stays in the Inbox.

## Create a project

To create a project:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Projects** in the sidebar. The **My Projects** page opens.</Step>
<Step>Click **Add** at the top right, then **Add project**.</Step>
<Step>Type a **Name** and pick a **Color**.</Step>
<Step>Switch on **Add to favorites** if you want the project in the sidebar's **Favorites**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Add**.</Step>
</Steps>

The project appears in the list and in the sidebar under **My Projects**. Click it to open it.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="P" /> to go to **My Projects** from anywhere.</Tip>

## Find your way around My Projects

**My Projects** lists every project with how many tasks are **Completed** and **Open**, and a progress bar. Type in **Search projects** to narrow the list. Switch on **Archived projects only** to see the projects you've archived.

To change a project from this page, hover over its row and click the three-dot button at the right. The menu has **Edit**, **Add to favorites**, **Archive** and **Delete**.

## Add tasks to a project

Open the project and click **Add task** at the bottom of the list, or at the bottom of a section. The new task belongs to that project, and to that section if you added it there.

From anywhere else, choose the project in the project menu at the bottom of Quick Add. To move a task you've already added, open it and change **Project** on the right.

Click **Add a description** under the project's name to note what the project is for. Click outside the box to save it.

## Split a project into sections

Sections group tasks inside one project, such as "Planning", "Shopping" and "Done this week". To add a section:

<Steps>
<Step>Open the project.</Step>
<Step>Click the three-dot button at the top right (**More project actions**), then **Add section**.</Step>
<Step>Type a name in **Name this section** and click **Add section**.</Step>
</Steps>

The section appears at the end of the project. To add one between two sections, hover over the gap between them and click the **Add section** line that appears.

Tasks without a section sit at the top of the project, above the first section. Click the arrow beside a section's name to fold it away.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="S" /> on a project page to add a section.</Tip>

### Rename, move into or delete a section

Hover over a section's name and click its three-dot button. The menu has **Edit**, to rename it, **Add section**, to add one below it, and **Delete**.

To move a task into a section, drag it there in the list or board. To use menus instead, open the task's page, click **Move task**, and pick the **Section**.

## Edit, archive or delete a project

Open the project and click the three-dot button at the top right (**More project actions**):

- **Edit** changes the name, colour and favourite setting.
- **Add to favorites** or **Remove from favorites** adds or removes it from the sidebar's **Favorites**.
- **Copy link to project** copies its address.
- **Archive** hides the project from the sidebar but keeps it and its tasks. The project page says "This project is archived."; click **Unarchive** to bring it back.
- **Delete** removes the project.

<Warning>Deleting a project also deletes its sections. Its tasks are kept and move to the Inbox.</Warning>

## Choose how a project looks

Each project has its own **Display** settings at the top right: a list, a board with a column per section, a calendar or a table. You can also sort, group and filter its tasks. The settings are saved with the project. See [Use the list, board, calendar and table layouts](/organise-views/use-the-list-board-calendar-and-table-layouts).
