A date tells Todalo when you plan to do a task, so it shows up in the Today view on that day and in the Upcoming view before then. You can type the date as part of the task's name or pick it from a calendar. A time is optional; without one, the task is for the whole day.

## Type a date into the task name

When you add a task, type the date the way you'd say it: `Call mom tomorrow at 4pm`. Todalo highlights the words it recognises. When you add the task, those words become its date and time and are taken out of the name, so this task is saved as "Call mom".

If a highlighted word belongs in the name, click it. It goes back to plain text for this task. If you pick a date from the **Date** chip instead, your pick wins and the words in the name are rewritten to match.

Typed dates work in Quick Add, in the **Add task** fields inside a list, and in Ramble. Editing a task's name in the open task doesn't read dates.

## Phrases Todalo recognises

| Type this | To get this |
| --- | --- |
| `today` or `tod` | Today |
| `tomorrow` or `tom` | Tomorrow |
| `friday` or `fri` | The next Friday, or today if today is Friday |
| `next week` | Monday of next week |
| `march 12` or `mar 12` | 12 March, next year if it has already passed |
| `12/3` or `12.3` | 12 March: the day comes first, then the month |
| `4pm`, `4:30pm` or `at 7:30pm` | That time |
| `19:30` or `at 19:30` | That time, on a 24-hour clock |
| `at 16` | 4 PM. `at` with a whole hour reads a 24-hour clock, so `at 4` is 4 AM |
| `2pm-3pm`, `from 2pm to 3pm` or `9:00 until 10:30` | A start and an end time |
| `19:30 for 30min` or `7pm for 1h 30min` | A start time and an end time that far after it |
| `every day`, `every 2 weeks`, `daily`, `weekly`, `monthly` | A repeat. See [Introduction to recurring dates](/tasks-capture/introduction-to-recurring-dates) |

A time without a date means today. Words that look like dates but aren't on this list, such as `the 12th` or `in 3 days`, stay in the name.

## Turn smart date recognition on or off

Smart date recognition is on for new accounts. To change it:

<Steps>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Click **General**.</Step>
<Step>Switch **Smart date recognition** off, or on again.</Step>
</Steps>

With it off, everything you type stays in the task's name, and Ramble doesn't read dates either. You can still pick dates from the **Date** chip.

## Pick a date from the calendar

To schedule a task you've already added:

<Steps>
<Step>Open the task.</Step>
<Step>Next to **Date** on the right, click the plus button (**Add date**), or click the date if there is one.</Step>
<Step>Pick a day on the calendar.</Step>
<Step>To give it a time, fill in **Start time**, and **End time** if you want a range. Leave both empty for an all-day task.</Step>
<Step>Click **Save**.</Step>
</Steps>

While you're adding a task, the **Date** chip under the name does the same, with shortcuts at the top: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Later this week**, **This weekend** and **Next week**.

<Tip>On a task in a list, press <Shortcut keys="T" /> to choose its date or <Shortcut keys="Shift+T" /> to clear it.</Tip>

## Change or remove a date

Open the task, click its date next to **Date**, and pick another day. To remove the date, click **Clear date** at the bottom of the same panel, then click **Save**. The task goes back to having no date.

To keep the day but drop the time, click **More actions** at the top right of the open task, then **Unschedule**. In a list, right-click the task and choose **Unschedule**.

## Set a deadline

A deadline is the day a task must be done by. It's separate from the date: you might plan to start a report on Monday (the date) for a deadline on Friday. The deadline shows on the task as "Deadline" with its day, and the task stays where its date puts it.

To set one, open the task and click the plus button next to **Deadline** (**Add deadline**), then pick a day and click **Save**. Click **Clear deadline** to remove it. While adding a task, use the **Deadline** chip.

<Tip>On a task in a list, press <Shortcut keys="D" /> to choose its deadline or <Shortcut keys="Shift+D" /> to clear it.</Tip>
