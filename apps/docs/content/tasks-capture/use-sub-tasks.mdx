"Paint the hallway" is hard to start; "buy masking tape" is not. Sub-tasks let you break a bigger task into the steps that make it up, keep them together, and see how far along you are.

## Add a sub-task

To add a sub-task:

<Steps>
<Step>Open the task you want to break down.</Step>
<Step>Under **Sub-tasks**, click **Add sub-task**.</Step>
<Step>Type the step, for example `Buy masking tape`. You can type a date into it, as in Quick Add.</Step>
<Step>Press Enter. The sub-task is added and the field stays open for the next one.</Step>
</Steps>

Press Esc when you've added them all. A sub-task always belongs to the same project as its parent task.

## Nest an existing task

If the task already exists, you can move it under another task:

<Steps>
<Step>Open the task, click **More actions** at the top right, then **Open task page**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Nest task**.</Step>
<Step>Choose the task to put it under from **Parent task**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Nest task**.</Step>
</Steps>

To take a sub-task back out one level, click **Unnest task** on its task page.

<Tip>In a project sorted by hand, press <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+]" /> to nest a task under the task above it, and <Shortcut keys="Ctrl+[" /> to unnest it.</Tip>

## How many levels you can nest

A sub-task can have sub-tasks of its own, up to three levels below the top task. Once a task is three levels down, **Add sub-task** no longer appears in it.

## Follow your progress

In a list, a task with sub-tasks shows a count such as "2/5": two of its five sub-tasks are done. Click the count to show or hide the sub-tasks under the task.

In the open task, the **Sub-tasks** heading shows the same count with a small progress ring. Completed sub-tasks move below **Add sub-task**. Click **Hide completed** to tidy them away, and **Show completed** to bring them back.

The open task also shows the parent of a sub-task above its name, with the parent's own count. Click it to open the parent.

<Tip>On a task in a list, press <Shortcut keys="Shift+E" /> to show or hide its sub-tasks.</Tip>

## Complete or delete a task with sub-tasks

Complete a sub-task by clicking its circle, in the list or in the open task. Each sub-task is completed on its own: completing the parent task doesn't complete the steps under it.

<Warning>Deleting a task also deletes all of its sub-tasks, and there is no undo.</Warning>
