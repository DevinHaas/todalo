A thought you don't write down straight away is easy to lose. Quick Add opens over whatever you're doing, takes one line, and puts the task where it belongs. You can type the date into the name and set everything else from the chips below it.

## Add a task with Quick Add

To add a task:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Add task** at the top of the sidebar, or press <Shortcut keys="Q" />.</Step>
<Step>Type the task in **Task name**, for example `Book a bike service Friday at 10am`.</Step>
<Step>Press Enter, or click the arrow button (**Add task**).</Step>
</Steps>

Todalo confirms with a line such as "Added “Book a bike service” to Inbox." Click **View task** to open it. Quick Add stays open and empty, so you can type the next task straight away. Press Esc, click **Close** or click outside Quick Add when you're done.

## Type the date into the name

With smart date recognition on, Todalo reads dates, times and repeats as you type and highlights them in the name. When you add the task, the highlighted words become its date and are taken out of the name. `Book a bike service Friday at 10am` is saved as "Book a bike service", due on Friday at 10 AM.

If a highlighted word belongs in the name, click it. It goes back to plain text for this task.

[Schedule a date and time](/tasks-capture/schedule-a-date-and-time) lists every phrase Todalo understands and shows how to turn recognition off.

## Set the details

The chips under the task name set everything else. Each one changes the task you're about to add:

- **Date** (shows **No Date** until you pick one): choose **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Later this week**, **This weekend** or **Next week**, or a day on the calendar. Once a date is set you can add a start and end time and a repeat.
- **Deadline**: the day the task must be done by.
- **Priority**: choose from **Priority 1 — highest** to **Priority 4 — normal**.
- **More options** (the plus button): **Add description**, and tick any of your **Labels**.

A chip you've set shows its value and a small cross. Click the cross (**Clear date** or **Clear deadline**) to remove it.

To file the task into a project, open the menu at the bottom left of Quick Add. It shows **Inbox** until you choose a project.

<Tip>Inside Quick Add, press <Shortcut keys="ArrowDown" /> to show the description, <Shortcut keys="Shift+ArrowDown" /> to show the chips, and <Shortcut keys="{" /> to choose a deadline.</Tip>

## Add a task inside a list

Every list has its own **Add task** button at the bottom. It opens the same fields inline, already set for where you are:

- In the Today view, the task is due today.
- In a project or a section, the task belongs to that project or section.
- In the Upcoming view, the task is due on the day you clicked.

Type the name and click **Add task**, or click **Cancel** to close it. This version shows **Add description** as a link under the name. In a project, a chip shows **Add at bottom**; click it to switch to **Add at top**.

<Tip>When a list has focus, press <Shortcut keys="A" /> to add a task at the bottom or <Shortcut keys="Shift+A" /> to add one at the top.</Tip>

## Start Ramble from Quick Add

When the task name is empty, the button at the bottom right of Quick Add shows a waveform (**Open Ramble**). Click it to speak your tasks instead of typing them. [Dictate tasks with Ramble](/tasks-capture/dictate-tasks-with-ramble) explains how.
