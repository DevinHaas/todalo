Some tasks never stay done: watering the plants, paying the rent, a weekly call. Instead of adding them again each time, give the task a recurring date. When you complete it, Todalo moves it on to its next date and keeps it in your list.

## How a recurring task works

A recurring date has three parts:

- How often: every so many days, weeks, months or years. Every day, every 2 weeks and every 3 months all fit.
- What it counts from: the task's date, or the day you complete it. The next section explains the difference.
- When it ends: never, or on a date you choose.

A recurring task shows a repeat icon next to its date in your lists. To see its rule, open the task and click its date: the rule is under **Repeat**.

When you complete a recurring task, it isn't ticked off. Its date moves on by one step, and it's back on your list on that day. Once a next date would fall after the end date, completing the task ticks it off for good.

## Repeat from the date or from completion

The difference matters when you're late. Say a task repeats every week and is due on Monday, and you complete it on Wednesday.

| Counts from | Next date | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scheduled date** | The Monday after | Things tied to the calendar: bins, rent, a weekly meeting |
| **Completed date** | The Wednesday after | Things that should happen a set time after you last did them: watering plants, a haircut, changing a filter |

With **Scheduled date**, a task you're late with keeps its rhythm, so it can come due again soon after you complete it. With **Completed date**, the gap after each completion stays the same, however late you were.

Every repeat counts from the scheduled date unless you choose **Completed date** in a custom repeat.

## Type a repeat into the task name

With smart date recognition on, you can type the repeat as part of the name while you add the task:

| Type this | To get this |
| --- | --- |
| `every day` or `daily` | Every day |
| `every week` or `weekly` | Every week |
| `every month` or `monthly` | Every month |
| `every year` | Every year |
| `every 3 days`, `every 2 weeks`, `every 6 months` | Every so many days, weeks, months or years |

Add a date to set where it starts: `Pay rent every month 1.12` repeats monthly from 1 December. A typed repeat always counts from the scheduled date.

<Note>Give a recurring task a date as well. A repeat without a date has nothing to count from, so the task waits in your list until you first complete it.</Note>

Todalo doesn't understand weekday rules such as `every monday` or `every weekday`. To repeat on Mondays, set the date to a Monday and choose a weekly repeat.

## Choose a repeat while adding a task

To set a repeat from Quick Add:

<Steps>
<Step>Type the task, then click the **Date** chip under the name.</Step>
<Step>Pick the first date.</Step>
<Step>Under **Repeat**, choose **Every day**, the weekly option (for example **Every week on Friday**) or the monthly option (for example **Every month on the 12th**).</Step>
<Step>Press Enter to add the task.</Step>
</Steps>

For every so many days, type the number next to **Every**, then click **Set**. For anything else, click **Custom…**.

## Set a custom repeat

A custom repeat lets you count from completion, repeat in weeks, months or years at any interval, and set an end date. To set one:

<Steps>
<Step>In Quick Add, click the **Date** chip, then **Custom…**.</Step>
<Step>Under **Based on**, choose **Scheduled date** or **Completed date**.</Step>
<Step>Next to **Every**, type a number and choose day, week, month or year.</Step>
<Step>Under **Ends**, choose **Never**, or **On date** and pick an **End date**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Set**.</Step>
</Steps>

To give an existing task a custom repeat, move to it in a list with the arrow keys and press <Shortcut keys="Mod+E" />. The task opens in the same card as Quick Add, with the same **Date** chip.

## Change or remove a recurring date

Open the task and click its date next to **Date**. Under **Repeat**, choose **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**, or **Does not repeat** to stop the task repeating. Then click **Save**.

A task with a custom repeat shows its rule in the same list. To change a custom rule, edit the task with <Shortcut keys="Mod+E" /> and choose **Custom…** again.

<Note>The repeat choices in the open task always count from the scheduled date. To repeat from completion, use a custom repeat.</Note>
