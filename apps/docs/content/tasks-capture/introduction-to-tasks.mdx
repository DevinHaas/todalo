Everything you plan in Todalo comes down to tasks. A task can be as small as `Buy milk` or carry a date, a deadline, a priority, labels, sub-tasks and comments. You only add the details you need, and you can add them later.

## Create a task

The quickest way to add a task is Quick Add, which works from every screen. To add a task:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Add task** at the top of the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Type the task in **Task name**, for example `Order paint samples`.</Step>
<Step>Press Enter. The task is added to the Inbox, or to the project you picked at the bottom of Quick Add.</Step>
</Steps>

You can also add a task where you're looking. At the bottom of the Inbox, the Today view or a project, click **Add task**, type the name and click **Add task** again. A task you add in the Today view is due today, and a task you add in a project belongs to that project.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Q" /> to open Quick Add from anywhere. [Use Quick Add](/tasks-capture/use-quick-add) covers everything you can set while you type.</Tip>

## Open a task

Click a task's name to open it. The task opens in a panel over your list, with its name and description on the left and its details on the right.

{/* screenshot: task-sheet */}

The right-hand side lists the task's properties:

- **Project**: where the task lives. Choose **Inbox** or one of your projects.
- **Date**: when you plan to do it, with an optional time and repeat. See [Schedule a date and time](/tasks-capture/schedule-a-date-and-time).
- **Deadline**: the day it must be done by, kept apart from the date you plan to do it.
- **Priority**: from **Priority 1 — highest** to **Priority 4 — normal**. See [Set a priority](/tasks-capture/set-a-priority).
- **Labels**: tick any of your labels.

An empty property shows a plus button. Click it (for example **Add date** or **Add deadline**) to set it.

If you use goals, the panel also shows **Horizon**, **Goals** and **Milestone**. [Introduction to goals](/goals-seasons-rituals/introduction-to-goals) explains them.

Below the description you'll find the task's **Sub-tasks** and **Comments**. See [Use sub-tasks](/tasks-capture/use-sub-tasks) and [Add comments and files to tasks](/tasks-capture/add-comments-and-files-to-tasks).

## Edit a task

To change a task's name or description:

<Steps>
<Step>Open the task.</Step>
<Step>Click the name or the **Description** field and type your changes.</Step>
<Step>Click **Save**, or press Enter in the name. Click **Cancel** to go back to what was there before.</Step>
</Steps>

Changes to the date, deadline, priority and labels wait for **Save** too. Moving the task to another project, adding a sub-task and adding a comment are saved straight away.

To leave the panel, click **Close** (the cross at the top right) or press Esc. Use **Previous task** and **Next task**, the arrows next to it, to move through the list you opened the task from. Todalo saves your changes first.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Mod+E" /> on a task in a list to edit it in place, in the same card as Quick Add.</Tip>

<Note>If you change a task on one device while it's open on another, Todalo doesn't overwrite either change. When you save, it says the task changed and offers **Load current details** or **Keep my changes**.</Note>

## Complete a task

When you've done something, click the circle to the left of its name. You can do this in a list or in the open task. The task is ticked off and leaves your list.

A repeating task doesn't leave the list. It moves on to its next date instead. [Introduction to recurring dates](/tasks-capture/introduction-to-recurring-dates) explains how the next date is worked out.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="E" /> to complete the task you've moved to with the arrow keys.</Tip>

## Use the task menu

Right-click any task in a list, on a board or in the calendar to act on it without opening it. The menu offers **Open**, **Copy link**, **Priority**, **Complete** and **Delete task**, among others.

In the open task, the same actions sit behind **More actions**, the three dots at the top right. From there you can also choose **Open task page** to see the task on a page of its own, which is where any files attached to it are listed.

## Delete a task

To delete a task:

<Steps>
<Step>Open the task.</Step>
<Step>Click **More actions** at the top right.</Step>
<Step>Click **Delete task**. The task is deleted and the panel closes.</Step>
</Steps>

You can also point at a task in a list and click the cross at the right of the row.

<Warning>Deleting a task also deletes its sub-tasks and comments, and there is no undo. If you're done with a task, complete it instead.</Warning>
