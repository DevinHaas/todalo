After a meeting or a weekly review you often have a handful of tasks to add, or a dozen to tidy at once. Todalo keeps Quick Add open between tasks, and lets you select tasks in a list and change them all in one go.

## Add several tasks in a row

Quick Add stays open after each task, so you can keep typing:

<Steps>
<Step>Press <Shortcut keys="Q" /> or click **Add task** at the top of the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Type the first task and press Enter.</Step>
<Step>Type the next task and press Enter. Repeat for each one.</Step>
<Step>Press Esc when you're done.</Step>
</Steps>

The **Add task** fields at the bottom of a list work the same way: after each task, the field clears and waits for the next one, which goes below the task you just added.

If you'd rather talk than type, [Ramble](/tasks-capture/dictate-tasks-with-ramble) lets you say a whole list of tasks and add them together.

<Tip>When a list has focus, press <Shortcut keys="A" /> to start adding at the bottom, or <Shortcut keys="Shift+A" /> at the top.</Tip>

## Select tasks

To select tasks in a list:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Select** at the top of the list.</Step>
<Step>Tick the box at the left of each task you want.</Step>
</Steps>

The toolbar shows how many tasks you've selected, for example "3 selected". **Select** is in the Today view, the Inbox and projects, the Upcoming view, labels and filters, and search results. In a project or in Upcoming it only shows in the list layout.

<Tip>Move to a task with the arrow keys and press <Shortcut keys="X" /> to select it. Press <Shortcut keys="," /> to jump to the toolbar.</Tip>

## Change the selected tasks

The toolbar acts on every selected task:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Complete** | Completes the tasks. Recurring tasks move on to their next date |
| **Delete** | Deletes the tasks and their sub-tasks |
| **Priority** | Sets one priority on all of them |
| **Labels** | Sets the labels on all of them |
| **Deadline** | Sets one deadline on all of them, or clears it if you leave it empty |
| **Horizon** | Moves them to Now, Next or Later |
| **Move** | Moves them to another project or section |
| **Clear date** | Removes the date from all of them |

**Priority**, **Labels**, **Deadline**, **Horizon** and **Move** open a short dialog. Make your choice and confirm it. The dialog starts from the first selected task's value.

<Note>**Labels** replaces the labels on every selected task with the ones you tick. To add one label without losing others, change the tasks one at a time.</Note>

<Warning>**Delete** deletes the selected tasks straight away, with no confirmation and no undo.</Warning>

## Finish selecting

Click **Clear selection** in the toolbar to untick everything, or **Done** at the top of the list to leave selection mode. Tasks you've completed, deleted or moved away drop out of the selection on their own.

If one of the tasks was changed or deleted on another device in the meantime, Todalo doesn't overwrite it and tells you what it couldn't change.
