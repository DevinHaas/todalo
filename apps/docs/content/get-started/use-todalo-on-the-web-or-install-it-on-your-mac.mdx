Todalo runs in your web browser and as an app for Mac. Both are the same Todalo: the same screens, the same shortcuts and the same account, so you can start a list at your desk and pick it up anywhere else. Use whichever suits you, or both.

## Use Todalo on the web

There's nothing to install. To open Todalo in your browser:

1. Go to [todalo.app](https://todalo.app).
2. Sign in, or create an account if you don't have one yet. See [Create your account and sign in](/get-started/create-your-account-and-sign-in).

Your tasks open in the Today view. Bookmark the page, or keep it in a pinned tab, so Todalo is one click away.

<Tip>Links to a task work in the browser too. Right-click a task, choose **Copy link** and paste it anywhere; opening it takes you straight to the task.</Tip>

## Install Todalo on your Mac

The Mac app is available from the Mac App Store. To install it:

1. Open the App Store on your Mac.
2. Search for `Todalo`.
3. Click **Get**, then **Install**. You may need to confirm with your Apple Account password or Touch ID.
4. Open Todalo from your Applications folder or from Launchpad.
5. Sign in with the same email and password, or Google account, that you use on the web.

<Note>The Mac app needs macOS 12 Monterey or later.</Note>

Keep Todalo in the Dock so you can open it with one click: while it's open, Control-click its icon in the Dock and choose **Options** › **Keep in Dock**.

## Use the same account everywhere

Your tasks belong to your account, not to the device. Sign in with the same account on the web and on your Mac, and everything you add, complete or move on one shows up on the other.

To check that you have the latest changes, click your name at the top left of the sidebar and look at **Sync**. It shows when Todalo last synced; click it to sync now.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Mod+R" /> to sync now from anywhere in the app.</Tip>

If your connection drops, Todalo tells you: "You're offline. Changes can't be saved until you reconnect." Your tasks stay on screen, and you can carry on once you're back online.

## What's different on Mac

Almost everything works the same on the web and on Mac. The differences are where Todalo meets your Mac itself:

| On the web | On Mac |
| --- | --- |
| **Continue with Google** signs you in within the same browser tab. | **Continue with Google** opens your default browser. You finish there, then return to Todalo. |
| Your session is kept by your browser. | Your session is stored in your Mac's Keychain. |
| **Help & resources** opens the help center in a new tab. | **Help & resources** opens the help center in your default browser. |
| Your browser asks before Ramble uses the microphone and before Ritual notifications appear. | macOS asks before Ramble uses the microphone and before Ritual notifications appear. |

On Mac, macOS asks for permission the first time Todalo needs the microphone or wants to show a notification. If you said no, or the prompt never appeared, see [Allow notifications and the microphone on Mac](/apps-account/allow-notifications-and-the-microphone-on-mac).

## Sign out

To sign out on either platform, click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Log out**. On Mac, this also removes your session from the Keychain.
