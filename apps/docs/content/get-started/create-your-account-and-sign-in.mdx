Your Todalo account keeps your tasks, projects and settings, and makes them the same on every device you sign in on. You can create it with an email address and a password, or with your Google account. Either way it takes under a minute.

## Create an account with your email

To create an account:

<Steps>
<Step>Open Todalo on the web or on your Mac. You see **Sign in**.</Step>
<Step>At the bottom, next to "Don't have an account?", click **Sign up**.</Step>
<Step>Enter your **Name**, **Email** and a **Password** of at least 8 characters.</Step>
<Step>Click **Sign up**.</Step>
</Steps>

Todalo signs you in straight away and asks once whether you'd like to bring your tasks from Todoist. Click **Import from Todoist** to start, or **Skip** if you don't use Todoist; see [Bring your tasks from Todoist](/get-started/bring-your-tasks-from-todoist).

If Todalo shows "Could not create your account. Check your details or sign in if you already have an account.", that email may already have an account. Click **Sign in** at the bottom and sign in instead.

## Sign in with your email

To sign in:

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Click **Sign in**.

Todalo opens the page you asked for, or the Today view.

## Sign in with Google

You can use your Google account instead of a password. The first time you do, Todalo creates your account from your Google name and email.

<Platform on="web">
1. On the sign-in screen, click **Continue with Google**.
2. Choose your Google account and allow access.
3. Google sends you back to Todalo, signed in.
</Platform>

<Platform on="mac">
1. On the sign-in screen, click **Continue with Google**. Your default browser opens, and Todalo shows "Continue in your browser, then return to todalo."
2. In the browser, choose your Google account and allow access.
3. When the browser shows the page to return to Todalo, click it to open the app. If your browser asks whether to open Todalo, allow it.
4. Todalo signs you in.

Changed your mind? Click **Cancel** in Todalo while it's waiting.
</Platform>

<Note>If you already have a Todalo account with a password for the same email, Todalo won't merge the two on its own. You'll see "This email already has a todalo account. Sign in with your password, then link Google from your account." Follow the next section to link them.</Note>

## Link Google to an account that has a password

Linking lets you sign in with either your password or Google. To link your Google account:

<Steps>
<Step>Sign in with your email and password.</Step>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Settings**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Calendars**.</Step>
<Step>Click **Link another Google account**, and finish in Google as you would when signing in.</Step>
</Steps>

From then on, **Continue with Google** signs you in to this account. Linking also connects that Google account's calendars; you can choose which ones show in Todalo from the same screen.

## Sign out

Click your name at the top left of the sidebar, then click **Log out**. You can also sign out from **Settings** › **Account**.

Signing out on one device doesn't sign you out anywhere else.

## If you can't sign in

- **"Could not sign in. Check your email and password."** Check for typing mistakes, and that you're using the email you signed up with. If you signed up with Google, use **Continue with Google** instead.
- **"Cannot reach todalo. Check your connection and try again."** Todalo couldn't reach its server. Check that you're online and try again.
- **"Google sign-in took too long. Try again."** The Google step expired before you finished it. Start again from **Continue with Google**.

You can't reset a forgotten password from the sign-in screen. Write to us at the address below and we'll help you back in. For more cases, see [Troubleshoot sign-in](/apps-account/troubleshoot-sign-in).
