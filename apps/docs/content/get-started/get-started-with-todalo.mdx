A to-do list only helps if getting things into it is quicker than keeping them in your head. Todalo lets you capture a task in a few keystrokes, type its date in plain words, and then shows it on the right day. This guide walks you through your first few minutes, from the sidebar to your first project.

## Find your way around

Everything starts from the sidebar on the left. From top to bottom you'll find:

- **Your name**: opens the account menu with **Settings**, **What's new**, **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Send feedback** and **Log out**.
- **Add task**: opens Quick Add, the fastest way to capture a task. The waveform button beside it (**Open Ramble**) lets you speak tasks instead.
- **Search**: finds tasks, projects and sections.
- **Inbox**: where new tasks land when you haven't given them a project or a date.
- **Projects**: all your projects, under **My Projects**.
- **Today**: what's due today, with anything overdue at the top.
- **Upcoming**: the days ahead, so you can see what's coming.
- **Filters & Labels**: your own views of tasks, built from labels and filter queries.
- **Help & resources**: opens this help center.

If you use goals, the sidebar also lists your **Goals**, the current Season and your **Rituals** above these views.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="M" /> to hide or show the sidebar.</Tip>

## Add your first task

Quick Add works from every screen. To add a task:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Add task** at the top of the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Type what you need to do in **Task name**, for example `Book the dentist`.</Step>
<Step>To file it straight into a project, choose one from the project menu at the bottom of Quick Add. It shows **Inbox** until you pick a project.</Step>
<Step>Press Enter, or click the arrow button (**Add task**).</Step>
</Steps>

Todalo confirms with "Added “Book the dentist” to Inbox." Click **View task** to open it, or keep typing to add the next one. Click outside Quick Add, or press Esc, to close it.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Q" /> to open Quick Add from anywhere in the app.</Tip>

You can also add a task inside a list. At the bottom of the Inbox, Today or a project, click **Add task**, type the name and click **Add task** again. A task you add in the Today view is due today.

## Type a date into the task name

You don't need a date picker to schedule a task. Type the date as part of the name and Todalo recognises it:

1. Open Quick Add and type `Call mom tomorrow at 4pm`.
2. Check the highlighted words. "tomorrow at 4pm" is highlighted because Todalo has read it as the due date and time.
3. Press Enter. The task is saved as "Call mom", due tomorrow at 4 PM.

Repeating dates work the same way: `Water the plants every day` creates a task that comes back each day.

If a word is highlighted but belongs in the name (a book called "Monday", say), click the highlighted words. They go back to plain text for this task.

<Note>Smart date recognition is on for new accounts. To turn it off, click your name, then **Settings** › **General**, and switch off **Smart date recognition**.</Note>

## Complete a task

When you've done something, click the circle to the left of the task. The task is ticked off and leaves your list. A repeating task moves on to its next date instead.

## Plan with Today and Upcoming

Once your tasks have dates, two views do most of the planning for you:

- **Today** lists everything due today. Tasks whose date has passed sit under **Overdue** at the top, so nothing slips quietly. The number beside **Today** in the sidebar counts what's still open.
- **Upcoming** shows the days ahead, so you can see a busy day coming and move tasks before it arrives.

The Inbox holds the tasks without a date or a project. Treat it as a holding area: go through it now and then, and give each task a date, a project, or both.

## Organise with projects

Projects group tasks that belong together, such as "Home", "Garden" or "Work". To create a project:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Projects** in the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Click **Add**, then **Add project**.</Step>
<Step>Type a **Name** and pick a **Color**. Switch on **Add to favorites** to keep the project at the top of the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Click **Add**.</Step>
</Steps>

New tasks you add inside the project belong to it. To move an existing task, open it and choose the project from its project menu.

## Add goals when you're ready

Todalo can also link your tasks to goals, plan goals in Seasons and walk you through optional Rituals such as a morning Check-in. You don't need any of it to use Todalo as a to-do list. If you'd rather not see goals at all, click your name, then **Settings** › **General**, and switch off **Show goals and seasons**. Projects and tasks work the same either way.

The [Todalo glossary](/get-started/todalo-glossary) explains each of these terms in a sentence or two.
