The Coach never edits your tasks directly. Whatever it proposes arrives as a draft: a set of changes that wait, marked as provisional, until you decide. Nothing in a draft touches your tasks or schedule until you accept it, and you can keep some changes and drop the rest.

## Where drafts appear

- In the Coach bar: when the Coach drafts changes from something you asked, a summary such as "4 added, 1 moved, 1 removed" appears above the bar. While the bar is closed, it stays beside the Coach button.
- In the Plan sidebar: **Draft my day with the Coach** on the Today view and **Draft the week with the Coach** beside the week calendar show the draft in a **Coach draft** box. **Update schedule** on a goal's recommendation drafts the week the same way.
- In a planning session: the plan for a goal appears in the planning dialog.

While a change waits, you also see it in place. A task that would move has a dashed outline and a chip with its new time. A task that would come off the schedule is struck through and shaded. A task to complete shows ticked and faded, and a task to delete shows red and struck through. New tasks, sub-tasks and attachments appear as dashed rows and chips, and on the calendar as dashed blocks. New tasks without a project show in the Inbox.

## Decide in the Coach bar

The summary has buttons to decide everything at once: the cross (**Decline all changes**) and the tick (**Accept all changes**). To go through the changes instead:

<Steps>
<Step>Click the summary. The changes open above the bar, grouped by kind, with the Coach's explanation at the top.</Step>
<Step>Click a group, such as **Added** or **Moved**, to see its changes. Each shows its task, what would change and the Coach's reason.</Step>
<Step>Click the tick beside a change to accept it, or the cross to decline it. The tick and cross beside a group decide the whole group.</Step>
<Step>Repeat until nothing is left. The summary disappears once every change is decided.</Step>
</Steps>

To change a draft, ask the Coach in the bar, for example `Make the essay block an hour later` or `Drop the gym session`. The Coach revises the waiting changes instead of adding new ones.

<Warning>Accepting a change that deletes tasks asks you to confirm first, for example "Delete 3 tasks?". Deleting can't be undone, and sub-tasks are deleted with their task.</Warning>

## Decide in the Plan sidebar

To have the Coach draft today's plan:

<Steps>
<Step>Open the Today view in the **Calendar** layout, so the Plan sidebar shows beside the schedule.</Step>
<Step>Click **Draft my day with the Coach**. Todalo shows "Drafting your day…".</Step>
<Step>Read the **Coach draft** box: a short summary, then one row per change with its time and reason.</Step>
<Step>Click the tick or cross beside each change, or click **Accept all** or **Decline all**.</Step>
</Steps>

Click **Draft again** for a fresh draft. It replaces the one you haven't decided yet. For the week, open the week calendar on the Upcoming view and click **Draft the week with the Coach**. The Coach gives your primary goals the best time; secondary-goal work gives way when time is short.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Alt+Shift+K" /> to accept the draft shown, or <Shortcut keys="Alt+Shift+X" /> to decline it.</Tip>

## Kinds of change

| Group | What accepting does |
| --- | --- |
| **Added** | Adds a new task, or puts a task at a time on your schedule |
| **Moved** | Changes a task's day or time, or moves it to another project or section |
| **Removed** | Takes a task off the schedule. It keeps its day |
| **Sub-tasks** | Adds a sub-task under a task |
| **Attached** | Attaches a document you gave the Coach to a task |
| **Completed** | Completes a task |
| **Deleted** | Deletes a task and its sub-tasks |
| **Re-sorted** | Moves a task to Now, Next or Later |
| **Kept** | Leaves the task as it is and counts it as fresh for another 30 days (from a tidy-up) |

A milestone from a planning session shows "Milestone, due" and its date, with its done criteria listed below.

## Schedule tasks that don't fit

When the day or week is too full, the draft ends with a question such as "3 tasks don't fit today. Schedule them tomorrow?" and lists those tasks.

- Click **Yes** to have the Coach draft them onto the next day with room. That's a new draft, which you accept or decline as usual.
- Click **No** to leave them where they are.

## When a change can't be applied

If you edit a task after the Coach drafted a change to it, that change is left out when you accept. Todalo says how many changes were left out. If every change was affected, nothing is accepted and Todalo asks you to review the task and try again. Draft again to get changes based on the task as it is now.
