Over a season, the Coach notices how you work, such as when you tend to finish goal work or which tasks keep getting moved. It writes each of these down as a Pattern, a short sentence you can read, correct or delete. The Coach uses only the patterns you can see, so you stay in control of what it assumes about you.

## How the Coach learns patterns

The Coach learns when you finish a Weekly review, or when you click **Learn now**. Each time, it looks at the current season only: your goal tasks that you completed, scheduled or moved, and the progress ratings you gave each goal. It doesn't learn from your chat messages or documents.

Each time adds at most three new patterns, and the Coach keeps up to 20. To learn, you need a connected AI provider and a season under way, with some goal work done, scheduled or rated.

Every pattern is sent to your AI provider with each request the Coach makes, so it can take them into account: drafting your day or week, answering in the bar, planning sessions, goal explanations and recommendations.

## See your patterns

To see what the Coach has learned:

<Steps>
<Step>Click your name at the top left of the sidebar.</Step>
<Step>Click **Settings** › **Coach**.</Step>
<Step>Scroll to **Patterns**, below the providers.</Step>
</Steps>

Each pattern shows its sentence, the evidence the Coach based it on, and where it came from, for example "Learned 12 Mar · Season from 1 Mar, weeks 1–2: goal tasks done, scheduled and moved, and progress ratings". Until there are any, Todalo shows "No patterns yet."

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="G" /> then <Shortcut keys="C" /> to open Settings › Coach from anywhere.</Tip>

## Edit a pattern

If a pattern is close but not quite right, rewrite it. To edit one:

<Steps>
<Step>Click **Edit** beside the pattern.</Step>
<Step>Change the sentence. It can be up to 160 characters.</Step>
<Step>Click **Save**, or **Cancel** to keep it as it was.</Step>
</Steps>

The pattern then shows "edited by you" with the date. The Coach uses your wording from then on.

## Delete a pattern

To delete a pattern, click **Delete** beside it. It's removed straight away, without asking, and the Coach stops using it.

<Warning>A deleted pattern can't be brought back. The Coach may learn something similar again later, if your work keeps showing it.</Warning>

## Learn patterns now

You don't have to wait for the Weekly review. Click **Learn now** at the top of **Patterns**. When it's done, Todalo says how many patterns it learned, or why it learned none, for example "Not enough to learn from yet: complete, schedule or rate some goal work first."

When you already have 20 patterns, delete some to make room.

<Tip>Press <Shortcut keys="Alt+Shift+E" /> to learn patterns now.</Tip>

## Clear your conversation and documents

Besides patterns, Todalo keeps your conversation in the Coach bar and the documents you attach until you start a new conversation. To clear them, click the Coach sidebar button (**Coach sidebar**) at the top of the page, then the bin button (**New conversation**).

If you disconnect every AI provider, Settings › Coach still lists your patterns so you can delete them.
